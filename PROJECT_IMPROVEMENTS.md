# Project Improvement Ideas

This document collects ideas to help the Medic project grow while remaining lightweight.

## Brand and Self‑Advertisement

- Maintain a consistent logo and color scheme across the app and marketing materials.
- Share regular updates on social platforms to build a community around the project.
- Consider writing small blog posts or tutorials demonstrating how to use the app.

## Monetization Approaches

- Offer paid support or premium features while keeping the core project free.
- Provide custom branding options for organizations that want their own look.
- Accept donations or sponsorships from users who find the project helpful.

## Security Considerations

- Serve content only from trusted sources to reduce the risk of cross‑site scripting.
- Keep the backend free of unnecessary dependencies to minimize attack surface.
- Use HTTPS in production and enable strict transport security.

These suggestions can be expanded over time as the project evolves.
